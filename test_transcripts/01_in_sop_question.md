# Test 1 — In-SOP Questions

**Scenario:** Customer asks about Botox pricing, booking, and opening hours — all answerable from the SOP.

---

**You:** Hi, what does Botox cost?

**Aria [faq]:** Hi there! Our Botox treatments start from £200. This includes a free initial consultation with one of our qualified practitioners to discuss your goals and assess your suitability. Would you like to book a consultation?

While I have you here — do you mind if I ask a couple of quick questions so we can help you better?

---

**You:** How do I book an appointment?

**Aria [qualify]:** You can book an appointment directly through our website or via WhatsApp!

To help us get things set up for you, which treatment are you most interested in — Botox, Fillers, or would you like to start with a free consultation?

---

**You:** What are your opening hours?

**Aria [qualify]:** We are open Monday to Saturday, from 9am to 7pm, and we are closed on Sundays.

To help us get you booked in, which of our treatments are you most interested in — Botox, Fillers, or would you like to start with a free consultation?

---

**Session Summary:**

CUSTOMER INTENT
The customer was trying to find out the cost of Botox treatments, how to book an appointment, and the clinic's opening hours.

KEY DETAILS COLLECTED
- Expressed initial interest in Botox treatments.
- Inquired about booking methods and clinic availability.

SOP GAPS IDENTIFIED
None. The assistant was able to answer all of the customer's questions regarding pricing, booking options, and opening hours using the existing SOP.

ESCALATION STATUS
Not escalated.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ACTION
Follow up with the customer to assist them in scheduling their free initial consultation for Botox, directing them to the website booking link or initiating a booking sequence via WhatsApp.

QUALIFICATION SUMMARY
Warm lead. While no formal qualification questions were answered, the customer demonstrated high intent by asking about pricing, booking procedures, and clinic opening hours.
